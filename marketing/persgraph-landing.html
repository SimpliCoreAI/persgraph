How It Works

From scattered signals to usable daily intelligence

Ingest once, organize automatically, and turn calendar, inbox, notes, and tasks into one operating view you can actually use.

1

Capture

Feed PersGraph emails, calendar events, links, notes, travel plans, and tasks. Your always-on AI agent keeps collecting useful context so nothing important slips through the cracks.

2

Organize

PersGraph structures and ranks your data behind the scenes, turning scattered inputs into searchable context plus prebrief signals like bills due, follow-ups, what is worth checking, and the exact context your daily Morning Brief can actually use.

3

Act

Use simple commands and daily briefings to ask questions, review schedules, catch pending follow-ups, and run your life from one interface — without hunting through inboxes, notes apps, and tabs.

Performance

Built to be cheap to run

Most AI tools burn API credits on every little thing. PersGraph is engineered to spend only when it has to.

🧭

Tiered Model Routing

Every task is matched to the cheapest model capable of handling it. Heartbeats and cron jobs never touch Sonnet.

TaskModelCost
Cron / heartbeats / morning briefing claude-haiku-4-5 ~20× cheaper
RAG search qwen2.5 local $0.00
Vector embeddings nomic-embed local $0.00
Reasoning / planning claude-sonnet Only when needed
💡

Zero-Cost Retrieval Pipeline

The most-used operation in the system — search and retrieval — runs entirely free on your own hardware.

Privacy First
Your data never
leaves your machine

PersGraph runs on infrastructure you own and control. Gru, the AI agent, runs 24/7 on your private VPS — no Mac required. LLMs and vector search run on your Windows machine via Tailscale, and sensitive local files stay on your Mac. No shared cloud, no subscription SaaS sending your data anywhere. You own everything, always.

🔒
Always-on, Always Private
