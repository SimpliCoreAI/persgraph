How It Works

Simple pipeline, powerful results

Three steps from raw data to queryable intelligence.

1

Ingest

Drop emails, links, PDFs, notes, finance data — PersGraph pulls it in from every source automatically or on-demand.

2

Embed

Every piece of data is chunked and embedded locally using Ollama. No cloud, no third party ever sees your data.

3

Query

Ask anything in natural language via Telegram, terminal, or the web UI. Your personal AI answers from your own graph.
