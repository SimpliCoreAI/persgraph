🙅 Never Do These
Never tip — it's considered rude and offensive in Japan
Never eat or drink while walking (except at festival stalls)
Never talk on your phone on trains/subways — text only
Never put chopsticks upright in rice (funeral symbolism)
Never pass food chopstick-to-chopstick (funeral symbolism)
Don't raise your voice in public — it's a quiet culture
Don't step on tatami mat borders (bad luck, also disrespectful)
✅ Always Do These
Remove shoes when entering homes, some restaurants, and temples
Bow slightly when greeting or thanking someone
Use two hands when giving or receiving items (cards, money, gifts)
Queue properly — lines are sacred in Japan
Walk on the left side; stand on left on escalators (right in Osaka/Kyoto)
Carry a small bag for your own trash — public bins are rare
Pay with exact change when possible — it's appreciated
Say "Sumimasen" (すみません) to get attention politely — means "excuse me"
🎌 Good to Know
June is rainy season (tsuyu) — expect humidity and occasional heavy rain
Convenience stores (konbini) are incredible: ATMs, food, printing, medicine, everything
7-Eleven, FamilyMart, Lawson — all have English menus and international ATMs
Tattoos may restrict access to onsen (hot spring baths)
Many places are cash-only, especially smaller restaurants and street stalls
IC cards (Suica/PASMO) work on almost all trains, subways, and buses
JR Pass must be activated at the airport on arrival
